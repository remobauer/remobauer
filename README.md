## Remo Bauer

**Founder of [Traidingdesk](https://www.traidingdesk.com)**, the AI trading journal for MetaTrader 5 traders.

Most traders don't lose because of their strategy. They lose because of habits: breaking their own rules, revenge trades, closing winners too early. Traidingdesk reads a trader's history, finds the habits that cost money, shows the amount and helps to stop them, with an AI coach that checks back weeks later.

### What I do

- Build Traidingdesk end to end: product, code, AI agents and launch
- Web apps with TypeScript and Next.js, one encrypted database per account
- AI workflows that turn raw trading data into clear, measurable feedback
